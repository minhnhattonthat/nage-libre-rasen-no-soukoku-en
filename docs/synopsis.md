# Synopsis (for translators and reviewers)

Built from a read of the extracted script; no guide was used. Fill gaps from neighbouring lines, not invention.

## Premise
Nanju Town (南寿町). The Nage Statue (ナージュ像) at Nanju Shrine kept monsters away; since it was stolen, pig-like
and bat-like monsters raid the shopping street, eat crepes and run off with daikon. A Nanju High student council
poster asks for volunteers to search for it. Five Nanju High girls sign up: Ann because it's the right thing,
Itsuki because the student council president Tsukasa-kun might notice her. A door inside the shrine leads to a
parallel world, and the girls travel through other worlds (amusement park, beach, hot spring, school-town maps)
fighting monsters and rival schools.

## Who is who
- **Ann Akagi** (face 1): leader, energetic, says what she thinks. 私.
- **Itsuki Morino** (face 2): boy-crazy, lazy about chores ("たるい"), sharp-tongued, fangirls over Tsukasa-kun.
- **Makoto Matsunaga** (face 3): childish, sleepy, airheaded; calls herself "Makoto" in the third person.
- **Shinobu Kazama** (face 4): tomboy, uses オレ and rough endings (ぜ, だな); a fighter. Plain rough English.
- **Mio Tsukushi** (face 5): polite rich-girl speech (ですわ, ましょう); formal, a little prim.
- **Jin Kiryu**, **Dai Wajima**: boys; their role is set by the script (help in later stages).
- **Tsukasa** (司): Nanju High student council president; Itsuki's crush.
- **Kaoru Kanzaki** (神崎 薫): Bairin Academy student council president, granddaughter of the Kanzaki Group
  chairman; Bairin students hold a fortress where the statue is thought to be. A boss ("Defeat Kaoru Kanzaki").
- **Ryo Kurosawa** (黒沢 亮): Oka High student council president, the main villain: he controls other schools'
  students (Eiko Academy students call him 黒沢様 "Lord Kurosawa"), absorbs power, and has a flying battleship.
- Schools: Nanju High (heroines), Bairin Academy, Oka High, Daido High, Eiko Academy.
- Monsters talk in pig/bat noises with the meaning in parentheses: "Oink oink! (Box, box!)".

## Structure
- MAP86, MAP85: New Game intro (poster; later the ship cutscene). MAP01-MAP24: numbered stages ("Stage N Start",
  a Clear Condition box each, a party-wipe "Thank you for playing" message). MAPnnR: revisits of a map. MAP30-50,
  MAP80-84: later worlds, the battleship, the ending. Each stage has party lines per girl (L1/R1 changes who talks):
  the same JP for several girls gets that girl's voice in English.
